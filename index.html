About David Tao

David Tao, born on July 11, 1969, in Hong Kong, is a renowned Taiwanese singer-songwriter and music producer, known as the "Godfather of Mandarin R&B".

His music blends R&B, Soul, Jazz, Rock, and other elements, pioneering a new era in Mandarin pop music. He is not only an outstanding singer but also a versatile musician, proficient in songwriting, composing, arranging, and producing.

Musical Career

1997

Released his debut self-titled album "David Tao", featuring hits like "Flowing Sand" and "Heart In Chaos", marking the beginning of a new era for Mandarin R&B.

1999

Released his second album "I'm OK", which included classics like "Love is Simple" and "22", solidifying his position in the Mandarin music scene.

2002

Launched "Black Tangerine", showcasing a more mature musical style with timeless classics like "Lonely Season" and "Love Me or Him".

2005

Released "The Great Leap", continuing to explore the perfect fusion of R&B and Mandarin pop music.

2013

Launched "How Have You Been", returning to musical essence and demonstrating profound musical expertise.
