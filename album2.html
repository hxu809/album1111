I'm OK (2004)

Click anywhere to start playing music

Released in 2004, "I'm OK" marks a significant chapter in David Tao's musical journey. This album showcases his signature R&B style infused with personal reflections and emotional depth. The title track became an instant classic, resonating with listeners across generations.

The album explores themes of self-acceptance, personal growth, and the complexities of modern relationships. David Tao's soulful vocals and sophisticated production elevate each track, creating an intimate listening experience that connects deeply with audiences.

With hits like the title track "I'm OK" and other memorable songs, this album solidified David Tao's reputation as one of the most influential R&B artists in Mandarin pop music, earning widespread critical acclaim and commercial success.
